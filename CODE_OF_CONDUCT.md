# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Agent Terminal to be a welcoming, respectful community for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability. This code applies to everyone who participates in the project, including maintainers and contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Welcome questions and constructive feedback; focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for the impact of your words and actions.
- Respect others' privacy, boundaries, and different perspectives.
- Help keep discussions relevant, collaborative, and accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or derogatory comments based on personal characteristics or identity.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or other conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community channels. It also applies when someone represents the project in public or interacts with community members in connection with the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately through [Dani Akash's X profile](https://x.com/dani_akash_). Include what happened, when and where it happened, and any relevant links or context you feel comfortable sharing. Please avoid posting sensitive reports or personal information in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as they can, respect the privacy of those involved, and share information only as needed to address the report. Retaliation against someone who makes a report or participates in its review is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for applying this code consistently and may remove or edit content, request changes in behavior, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response will depend on the circumstances and severity of the conduct. Maintainers involved in a report should not make decisions about that report.

## Attribution

This code is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), a widely used open-source community code of conduct.
